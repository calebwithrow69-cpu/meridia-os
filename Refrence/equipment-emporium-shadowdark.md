# Equipment Emporium — Adapted for Shadowdark / Meridia OS

*Companion to `shadowdark-ruleset-compilation.md`. Source: Basic Fantasy RPG's Equipment Emporium (public domain / OGL).*

**Weapons and armor are deliberately excluded** — those are locked in the main ruleset file at Caleb's request to avoid balance drift.

## Conversion notes

**Prices** have been adjusted toward Shadowdark's grittier economy. Rough conversion rules used:
- Basic tools/consumables already in Shadowdark canon: use Shadowdark's price
- Premium goods (BFRPG had inflated pricing here): reduced ~30–40%
- Food and consumables: reduced ~50% (Shadowdark rations are ~1.7 sp/day; BFRPG meals were 3+ sp/day)
- Trade goods sold by bulk unit (metals per lb, cloth per yard): kept close to BFRPG, these are wholesale prices
- Services: kept close to BFRPG, adjusted for Shadowdark's coin values

**Weight → Gear slots** conversion:
- Items < 1 lb or marked * (BFRPG "10 per lb"): **1 slot holds 5–20 of these** (specified per entry)
- Items 1–5 lb: **1 slot each**
- Items 5–15 lb: **2 slots each**
- Items 15–30 lb: **3–4 slots**, considered *bulky*
- Items 30+ lb: **haul only** (cannot carry in adventure gear; needs cart, sled, or teamster)

**Shop tier tags** for Phase 5 stock generation:
- `[P]` Poor district (Gutterwash, Rilken Row)
- `[M]` Middle district
- `[U]` Upper district
- `[H]` High Harbor
- Multi-tier items list all tiers where they'd appear.

## 21. QUICK REFERENCE — WHAT GOES IN WHICH SHOP

For Meridia OS Phase 5, cross-reference by shop type:

**Filthy bakery [P]:** Cheap bread, dried fruit, grain, meal, cheap tavern fare
**Used adventuring gear [P]:** Second-hand torches, bedrolls, rope, iron spikes, hemp rope, damaged crowbars
**Corpse collector [P]:** Rags, shrouds, teeth (raw materials)
**Pawn shop / fence [P]:** Random items from any tier, always at 30–50% price, often stolen
**Moneylender [P]:** Services only
**Tannery [P]:** Bulk leather, common pelts, cured meat as byproduct
**Back-alley chirurgeon [P]:** Bandages, crutches, common substances, wolfsbane
**Ratcatcher [P]:** Rat catching services, sometimes trained ferrets or rat poison
**Fishmonger [P]:** Fresh/pickled fish, fishing gear, hooks
**Gambling house [P]:** Dice, cards, marked/loaded variants under the counter
**Drug den [P]:** Tobacco, pipeweed, exotic herbs, pipes

**Brewer [M]:** Barrel drinks by the barrel or tun
**Butcher [M]:** Cured meats, carcasses, salt, herbs
**Tailor [M]:** Common outfits, common cloth, individual clothing pieces
**Common blacksmith [M]:** Tools (hammers, chisels, pliers, chains), nails, horseshoes, cook implements
**Adventuring gear [M]:** Full Shadowdark canon list + rope, tinder, lantern, backpacks
**Leatherworker [M]:** Bulk/fine leather, leather outfits, scabbards, straps
**Shipwright/carpenter [M]:** Lumber, canvas, tar, tools
**Stonemason [M]:** Iron spikes, hammers, sledges
**Herald/town crier [M]:** Services (announcements, messenger work)
**Livestock [M]:** Farm animals, tack, feed

**Fine tailor [U]:** Rich cloth, courtier's/entertainer's outfits, silk pieces
**Glassblower [U]:** Mirrors, lenses, spectacles, glass vials, wine glasses
**Jeweler [U]:** All jewelry, gems, precious metals
**Apothecary [U]:** Alchemy glassware, herbalist kits, herbs, common through rare reagents, potions on rare stock
**Artist [U]:** Paintings, etchings, sculptures, native art
**Scribe [U]:** Books, ink, quills, paper/vellum, scroll cases, seals
**Guildhall [U]:** Services, trade goods on commission
**Goldsmith [U]:** Gold items, ornate jewelry, metals
**Master blacksmith [U]:** Quality craft tools, ornamental metalware, precious-metal items
**Antiques & curios [U/H]:** Historical artifacts, exotic art, statues, tapestries

**High Harbor exclusive [H]:** Royal regalia, exceptional wines/spirits, elaborate altar symbols, crystal chandeliers, exotic pets and mounts, flying mounts (by special order), master-crafted furnishings, giant gems
